## State-loss defect and scoped correction

The feature's three histories m_aiVoteAlignment, m_aiElectionTeamPreference and m_aiCityAssignmentPlayerPreference are allocated/reset to zero, then changed after votes and consulted by the preferred-ballot/candidate/city-recipient queries. Neither original nor current pre-patch CvPlayerAI::read/write stores any of the three arrays. Thus saving does not preserve this history, and loading starts from zero rather than the state that influenced preferences before the save. These are event-updated histories, not values reconstructed by the existing reader.
